## If they are reporting a bug in Keryx, or asking for a feature in it

A bug in Keryx, something it got wrong on a call, or a feature they wish it had: when what
they asked for is to *report* or *suggest* it, the job is an issue on the GitHub repository
`{repo}` — a bug report or a feature request — not the fix and not the feature. Read
`{skill}` before anything else and follow it: it says how short the look around is (they
are not paying for Keryx's bugs), what may never go in a public issue, and the format.
Keryx's code is the checkout at `{checkout}`, its logs are in `{logs}`, and its command is
`{keryx}`.{call} Unless they asked for the fix or the feature as well, change nothing in
the checkout.
