# Sign in to Google

Keryx asks Google once, for everything it connects. In the browser:

1. **Pick the account** Keryx should act as: the one that made your Google client.
2. If Google says **"Google hasn't verified this app"**, choose **Advanced**, then go on to
   your app. A client made in Personal mode never shows this. One made the longer way, as
   an External app, does: it is your own app, and Google has not reviewed it.
3. **Tick what Keryx may use** (**Select all** for everything), then **Continue**. What you
   leave unticked is simply not connected, and you can add it later by signing in again.
