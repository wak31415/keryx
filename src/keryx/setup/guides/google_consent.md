# Sign in to Google

Keryx asks Google once, for everything it connects. In the browser:

1. **Pick the account** Keryx should act as. With a client made in Personal mode, that is
   the account that made it. With one made the second way, it is the work or school
   account you made it for, not the account that owns the project.
2. If Google says **"Google hasn't verified this app"**, choose **Advanced**, then go on to
   your app. A client made in Personal mode never shows this. One made the second way
   does: it is your own app, and Google has not reviewed it. If Google says instead that
   your organization blocks the app, only its administrators can allow it.
3. **Tick what Keryx may use** (**Select all** for everything), then **Continue**. What you
   leave unticked is simply not connected, and you can add it later by signing in again.
