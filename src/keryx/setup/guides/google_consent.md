# Sign in to Google

Keryx asks Google once, for everything it connects. In the browser:

1. **Pick the account** Keryx should act as.
2. If Google says **"Google hasn't verified this app"**, choose **Advanced**, then
   **Go to Keryx (unsafe)**. Google shows that warning for every app until it has finished
   reviewing it, and Keryx's review is under way.
3. **Tick what Keryx may use** (**Select all** for everything), then **Continue**. What you
   leave unticked is simply not connected, and you can add it later by signing in again.
