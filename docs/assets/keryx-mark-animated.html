Keryx mark, animated

The mark in each state Keryx can be in. Three pieces and nothing else: they stay three distinct pieces in every state and morph between them.

Your device asks for reduced motion, so the looping motion is paused.
  • Idle / speaking — three vertical bars; while speaking they follow a speech-like level.
  • Listening — the bars squash down into one line, then each stretches to its full length and thins; the three trace the same voice line slightly out of step, its swing fading to almost nothing towards the ends.
  • Working — the three pieces bend into three arcs of a circle and spin, like a loader.
